Pipeline

1. Data

Reuse your merged episode table (labels plus MRI, AMY, TAU and FDG with availability flags).
Split by subject with 5-fold CV. Fit preprocessing on training folds only.

2. Characterise the joint imbalance (you already have most of this)

Class counts, availability per class, the class × pattern table, Cramér’s V, and the mutual information between class and pattern.
Check confounders: ADNI phase and scan year. Patterns like AMY or TAU may only exist in later phases, so “availability” may partly be “cohort era”. Say this explicitly.

3. Models (keep them few)

A tabular gradient-boosted model (LightGBM or XGBoost, which accepts missing values) as the strong baseline.
Your late-fusion MLP as the neural model.
Optional: logistic regression as a simple sanity baseline.

4. Core experiments (the contribution)

A. Shortcut probe: Train on the availability mask alone, with no imaging features. If it beats chance clearly, that confirms H1.
B. Mask ablation: Compare features + mask against features with the mask hidden, to see how much the model leans on availability.
C. Mitigation arms, each compared with the plain baseline:
class-weighted loss
joint reweighting, with weight proportional to P(class)·P(pattern) / P(class, pattern), which breaks the dependence between class and availability
random modality dropout during training
2 and 3 together
D. Balanced-availability evaluation: Also evaluate on a test set reweighted so that every class has the same pattern mix. The gap between natural and balanced results measures how much the scores depend on the availability pattern.

5. Evaluation

Overall: balanced accuracy, macro-F1, macro-AUROC and recall per class.
Group-level: balanced accuracy for each class × pattern group (ignore groups with fewer than about 50 samples), plus the worst-group accuracy and the best-minus-worst gap.
Subject-cluster bootstrap CIs, with results averaged over at least 3 seeds.

6. Analysis

Explain the variation in whether predictions are correct using a simple logistic regression: correct ~ class + pattern + class:pattern. This shows how much of the error comes from class, from pattern, and from their interaction. That is your “joint” result.
Add a heatmap of recall by class × pattern. It will be your headline figure.
