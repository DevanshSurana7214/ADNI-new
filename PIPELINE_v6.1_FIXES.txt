PIPELINE v6.1 - REVISION INSTRUCTIONS (follow after v6; do not restart from scratch)

======================================================================
0. WHY THIS REVISION
======================================================================
The v6 run executed correctly but several results are INVALID or UNINFORMATIVE
because of design/implementation problems, not because the hypotheses failed:

  P1  M4 and M4c are IDENTICAL to M1 on the dial and on BASELINE (all deltas
      0.0000 [0,0]). tau is picked on validation sets that contain no cell with
      n >= 30, so tau=0 is always selected. H2 and H4 therefore were not tested.
  P2  The MLP is failing: Dementia recall = 0.000 in several runs; balanced accuracy
      0.415 (BASELINE) and ~0.385 (dial) vs ~0.59 for LGBM. MLP gains from M2/M5 are
      mostly repairing a collapsed model.
  P3  H1 (harm grows with lambda x skew) was tested on T_matched macro cell accuracy.
      When availability is informative, a matched test IMPROVES with lambda (LGBM
      lambda coefficient +0.033). Harm appears when availability stops being
      informative, so the outcome must be T_indep and the gap T_matched - T_indep.
      Also the OLS used only 15 observations / 15 clusters (too little power).
  P4  The H3 verdict "SUPPORTED" contradicts the tables (M2 raises macro cell
      accuracy at lambda=0 and on phase-held-out). The verdict rule has no
      "inconclusive" state and ignores degenerate arms.
  P5  The DIAL cohort is small (1009 subjects, 11 cells); cell metrics are noisy.
  P6  natural_minus_balanced_gap is negative for every arm, including the mask-only
      probe M0 (-0.098), which is implausible; sign also differs from the earlier v5
      run. The balanced-availability evaluation may be mis-specified.
  P7  Spec deviations: natural runs use 1 seed (spec: 3); error-decomposition
      bootstrap n_boot=300 (spec: 2000); the +-90-day sensitivity still contains
      pattern 0000 episodes (should be dropped); local absolute paths in the report.
